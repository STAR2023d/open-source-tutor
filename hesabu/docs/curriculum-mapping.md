# Curriculum mapping

Source: KICD primary curriculum designs, Mathematics (Grade 4 design revised 2024).
Strands: Numbers, Measurement, Geometry, Data Handling.

Grade 4 sub-strands: Whole Numbers, Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, Division, Fractions,
Decimals, Use of Letters; Length, Area, Volume, Capacity, Mass, Time, Money; Position and
Direction, Angles, Plane Figures; Data.

Rules:
- One skill = one small, testable outcome inside a sub-strand (id: `g4.num.add.regroup`).
- `kicdRef` points at the design section. We reference, we never copy KICD text (it is copyrighted).
- Performance levels (EE/ME/AE/BE) in the app are indicative only, not KNEC grading.
